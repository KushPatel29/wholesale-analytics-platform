What this is

A server-rendered analytics suite for Northgate Retail Group, a fictional supercenter chain. Flask + Jinja on a DuckDB star schema over Parquet, with role-based access control and row-level scoping enforced in the query layer. All data is synthetic and generated by seed/generate_synthetic_data.py.

Worth a look: Overview for the executive summary and movement analysis, Customers for RFM and cohort retention, Products for margin and price-band work, and Regions for territory concentration. Every page shares one filter bar, one comparison-window definition and one metric catalogue.
